Script Shop Management

Run all your local shops from a single Odoo application: purchases, sales, stock and a modern dashboard for every shop.

Odoo 19.0 · Point of Sale · Purchase · Inventory · LGPL-3

What it does

Shops from your Points of Sale

Tick Is a Shop on any Point of Sale and give it an image. Each shop gets its own card and its own figures.

The right products in the right shop

Choose in which shops each product is available. A shop's cash register only loads and finds the products assigned to it, even with restricted categories.

Destination shop

Set the destination shop on purchase orders and stock transfers, to know exactly what is bought for, and delivered to, each shop.

A dashboard that reads at a glance

  • One card per shop, plus one card for all the shops with your company logo.
  • Period selector: today, this week, this month, this year or custom dates.
  • Indicators: purchases, sales, real turnover (tax excluded, net of refunds) and stock.
  • Turnover and purchases curves, turnover by shop, best-selling and least-selling products.
  • One click on an indicator opens the matching list, already filtered on the shop.

Purchases, Orders and Stock per shop

Three dedicated menus list only what concerns your shops, with quick search by shop and by product, filters and grouping.

Security and languages

  • User group: sees the dashboard and the shop lists.
  • Manager group: also sets up the shops (shop flag, image, products of each shop).
  • A shop must belong to the company of the purchase or transfer that uses it.
  • Available in English and French.

Getting started

  1. Install Script Shop Management (it needs Point of Sale, Purchase and Inventory).
  2. Add your user to the Script Shop Management group (User or Manager).
  3. Open your Point of Sale configuration, tick Is a Shop and add an image.
  4. On your products, fill Available in Shops.
  5. Open Script Shop Management › Dashboard.

Version française

Pilotez toutes vos boutiques physiques depuis une seule application Odoo : achats, ventes en caisse, stock et un tableau de bord moderne pour chaque boutique.

  • Boutiques : cochez Est une boutique sur un point de vente et ajoutez son image.
  • Produits par boutique : la caisse d'une boutique ne charge que les produits qui lui sont assignés.
  • Boutique de destination sur les achats et les transferts de stock.
  • Tableau de bord : une carte par boutique et une carte « Toutes les boutiques », sélecteur de période, achats, ventes, CA réel, stock, courbes, produits les plus et les moins vendus.
  • Achats, Commandes et Stock : listes dédiées avec recherche rapide par boutique et par produit.
  • Sécurité : groupes Utilisateur et Responsable. Disponible en français et en anglais.
